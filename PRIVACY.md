# Privacy Policy: Oura Workout Alert

_Last updated: October 19, 2026_

Oura Workout Alert is a free, non-commercial hobby project by Isabelle Laususan, shared with friends to try.

**How it runs: **each person runs their own copy in their own GitHub account. Your data goes from Oura to your copy and then to your phone. It never passes through the creator or any server she runs, and she can't see it.

**What it accesses: **workout data from the Oura API (activity type, time, duration, calories, distance and intensity). Nothing else.

**How it's used:** only to send you notifications about your own workouts and your progress against your weekly fitness goals.

**Where it's stored: **a small state file in your own GitHub repository. It holds your Oura login token and the IDs of workouts you've already been notified about. Workout data is not stored long-term.

**Sharing:** your data is never sold or shared. Notification text is sent through the ntfy.sh service to your phone, on a topic name you choose.

**Deleting your data: **revoke the app's access in your Oura account settings and delete your copy of the repository. This stops all access immediately.
